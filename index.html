Independent research and consultancy

XTC Training OÜ provides independent research and consultancy to individuals and organisations, enabling better-informed decisions through rigorous analysis and structured reporting.

Our services Get in touch

Services

Each engagement is defined in advance and scoped precisely to the question at hand. Research draws on publicly available sources and information supplied by the client.

Business & market research

Desk research into markets, sectors and competitors, synthesised into focused findings to support planning and decision-making.

Company & supplier background checks

Assessment of prospective partners and suppliers using public registers and published information, prior to commitment.

Due diligence support

Risk-aware research to inform transactions and the onboarding of customers or partners.

Research for individuals

Independent review of an investment opportunity, company or service provider based on public sources, delivered with a structured summary of findings.

Process & operations review

Practical examination of how work is carried out, informed by experience in operations, retail, warehouse and facilities management, with recommendations for improvement.

Training & workshops

Focused sessions for teams covering risk awareness, fraud awareness and sound operational process.

Electronic device development projects

Concept development, prototyping and project-based support for small electronic devices.

XTC Training OÜ provides research and general consultancy. It does not provide legal advice, regulated financial or investment advice, or private investigation services.

Approach

A disciplined and transparent method of working.

  1. Understand

    We establish what needs to be known, why it matters, and the precise scope of the work.

  2. Research

    Methodical research using reliable public sources and the information provided by the client.

  3. Report

    Evidence-based findings, explicit identification of what could not be confirmed, and practical next steps.

How we work with clients

  • A limited number of clients at any one time, with direct attention to each engagement.
  • Continued support where the work extends beyond a single report or workshop.
  • Ongoing awareness of relevant developments in technology and regulation.
  • A small core team, supplemented by external specialists when a project requires them.
  • Custom hardware and software development for electronic device projects, where this forms part of the brief.

About
